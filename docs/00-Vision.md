## Purpose

Build an **AI Software Factory** — a platform that turns a customer idea into a sellable simple digital product with minimal human involvement.

ASF is not “another AI coder”. It is a production line: Discovery → specification → planning → implementation → quality gates, with durable project memory and human decisions only where they matter.

## Success vision

A small business or internal stakeholder writes (or speaks) into Telegram:

> “I need a booking bot for my auto service.”

Within a short Discovery cycle they get a solid specification. After owner approval, the factory produces tasks and drives a working MVP for product types such as:

- website / landing
- Telegram bot
- REST service / internal tool
- light AI automation

## Non-goals (platform vision)

- Replacing humans on strategy, pricing, and legal accountability
- Fully autonomous enterprise delivery with zero review
- Competing on “more agents”; competing on **memory, process, and quality of requirements**

## Differentiator

The durable asset is the **Knowledge Graph / project memory**, not any single LLM or agent persona. Models will change; the factory’s process and memory should not have to be rewritten.
