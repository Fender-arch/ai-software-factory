# Product type: website

## Typical MVP

- Marketing / brochure site or simple landing
- Contact form or CTA
- Responsive layout
- Optional CMS-lite (static content in repo)

## Discovery checklist

- [ ] Audience and primary CTA
- [ ] Pages / sections
- [ ] Brand assets availability
- [ ] Form destinations (email, CRM, none)
- [ ] Languages
- [ ] Hosting expectation

## Out of scope for this template

Multi-tenant SaaS, complex auth, payments (escalate or narrow).
